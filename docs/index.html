Why this matters

Pakistan's Lady Health Worker Programme is one of the largest community health systems in the world — and it still runs on paper.

90,000+
Lady Health Workers active across Pakistan
~60%
of Pakistan's population served, mostly rural
20+

Source: Hafeez et al., “Lady health workers programme in Pakistan: challenges, achievements and the way forward”, J Pak Med Assoc (2011).

What happens in one exchange

Three steps, running inside a single spoken sentence.

Real-time listening

Handles Urdu, Punjabi, and English mixed in the same sentence, transcribed as she speaks.

Structured extraction

Patient name, symptoms, and vitals pulled out precisely — never guessed. Missing details trigger one short follow-up.

Danger-sign triage

Every visit is screened against a reviewed red-flag list. Serious cases are flagged immediately, before anything else.
